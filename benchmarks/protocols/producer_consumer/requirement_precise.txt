A single bounded buffer of fixed capacity N is shared by producers and
consumers.

A producer may add an item only when the buffer is not full. A consumer may
take an item only when the buffer is not empty. The buffer length must never
exceed N (no overflow) and must never go negative.

The system must not deadlock: if the buffer has free slots, a waiting producer
must eventually be able to proceed; if the buffer has items, a waiting consumer
must eventually be able to proceed. Producers and consumers are scheduled
fairly enough that a continuously enabled party is eventually run.

There is one buffer, capacity N is constant, and no other process writes the
buffer except through the producer and consumer operations.
