Five philosophers sit around a table with five forks. Philosopher i needs
fork i and fork (i+1) modulo 5 in order to eat.

After eating, both forks are released. Under that acquisition order the
system is deadlock-free: it is never the case that every philosopher holds
one fork and waits forever for the other.

Assume a fair scheduler so a philosopher who can acquire the next fork is
eventually scheduled. There are exactly five philosophers and five forks; no
other process picks up forks.
